ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 lists the characteristics that every individual requirement should have:

- **Necessary**: it states a capability or constraint that is really needed; removing it would leave a gap.
- **Appropriate**: its level of detail fits the level of the entity it belongs to (system, software, component) and
  does not impose design too early.
- **Unambiguous**: it can be read in only one way.
- **Complete**: it says everything needed to understand it, with no "to be determined" left.
- **Singular**: it states one requirement only, not several joined with "and".
- **Feasible**: it can be achieved within known technology, cost and schedule limits.
- **Verifiable**: there is a practical way (test, analysis, inspection or demonstration) to show it is met.
- **Correct**: it accurately reflects the stakeholder need it came from.
- **Conforming**: it follows the agreed template and style for requirements of the project.

A requirement that fails one of these characteristics should be rewritten before it is baselined.
