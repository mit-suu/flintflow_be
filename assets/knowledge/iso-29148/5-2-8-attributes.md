section: 5.2.8 Requirement attributes
topic: requirements
description: Attributes recorded with each requirement to support management and traceability

---

ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 recommends recording attributes alongside each requirement statement, so the requirement can be
understood, managed and traced. Common attributes are:

- **Identification**: a unique, stable identifier used for references and traceability.
- **Rationale**: why the requirement exists, which helps when it is challenged or changed.
- **Source / owner**: the stakeholder or document it comes from and who is responsible for it.
- **Priority**: how important it is relative to other requirements, used when scope must be cut.
- **Risk** and **difficulty**: how uncertain or hard it is to meet.
- **Stability**: how likely it is to change.
- **Type**: for example functional, performance, interface, quality or constraint.
- **Verification method**: how it will be shown to be met (inspection, analysis, demonstration or test), and later the
  verification status.

The project chooses which attributes it keeps; the identifier and the traceability to its source are the ones nearly
every project needs.
